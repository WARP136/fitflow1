Drop your downloaded LottieFiles .json animations in THIS folder, named exactly:

  pushup.json          (you have this)
  jumpingjacks.json    (you have this - rename it to remove spaces)
  jumpingsquats.json   (you have this - rename it to remove spaces)
  water.json           (you have this)
  stretch.json         (optional - onboarding)
  jogging.json         (optional - jog tracker empty state)
  celebrate.json       (optional - weekly wrap milestone)

Any file that is missing is handled gracefully: the app draws a CSS
fallback animation instead and never crashes. So ship with 4 or 7, both work.
